1. What Rydo is

Rydo is a platform that connects passengers with independent bike and rickshaw riders in and around Kohat. Rydo does not itself provide transport. Riders are independent and responsible for their own vehicles, licences and conduct.

2. Your account

  • You must give a real phone number and keep your account secure.
  • You may use guest mode to explore. Some features need you to log in.
  • You can delete your account at any time from the app.

3. Fares and offers

  • Rydo shows a suggested fare. You may offer your own fare, and riders may accept it or send a counter-offer.
  • The fare you accept is the agreed price for that trip. Extra waiting time or a changed destination may change it, and the app will show this.
  • Pay the rider in cash or by the method you choose in the app.

4. Passenger rules

  • Be at the pickup point on time and treat riders with respect.
  • Do not carry illegal, dangerous or prohibited items.
  • Cancel through the app if your plans change.

5. Rider rules

  • Riders must be adults with a valid licence, a road-worthy vehicle and approved documents.

  • Rydo may suspend riders for unsafe behaviour, fraud or repeated complaints.
