Rydo by Global Nova Technologies

Privacy Policy

App: Rydo (Android) · Developer: Global Nova Technologies, Kohat, Pakistan · Effective: 16 November 2026

This policy explains what information the Rydo app collects, why, who it is shared with, and the choices you have. We collect only what is needed to book and complete bike and rickshaw rides.

1. Information we collect

DataWhy we use itWhere it is kept
Name and phone number (when you log in)To create your account and show your name to riders on a booked rideOn your device
Location: the pickup point you set on the map and your destinationTo show nearby riders, calculate distance and fare, and run the rideOn your device. Your current trip is shared with the rider while the ride is active.
Ride history, favourite riders, emergency contacts, language and themeTo show your past rides and make booking fasterOn your device
Microphone audio, only while you hold the voice-booking buttonTo turn your spoken destination into textProcessed by your phone's speech service (see section 3). Rydo does not record or store audio.
Chat messages with your riderTo coordinate pickupInside the ride. Your phone number is not shared with the rider.
Rider applications (riders only): name, CNIC, licence, vehicle and emergency contact detailsTo verify riders before they can accept ridesReviewed by the Rydo team

We do not collect your contacts, photos, SMS, call logs or advertising ID. Rydo shows no ads and we never sell your data.

2. Permissions

  • Internet: to load maps, search places and run rides.
  • Microphone: only for voice booking, and only when you tap the mic. You can deny it and type instead.

3. Services that receive some data

  • Mapbox: shows the map. Your device requests map images for the area you are viewing (Mapbox privacy).
  • OpenStreetMap (Nominatim): finds places. The text you search or say and the approximate map area are sent to find matching places (OSMF privacy).
  • Your phone's speech recognition (usually Google): converts your voice to text when you use voice booking (Google privacy).
  • Your phone dialer: opens when you tap an emergency number such as 15. The call is made by your phone, not by Rydo.

4. Safety features

If you press SOS, Rydo alerts its safety team with your ride details, and you can share your live trip with your emergency contacts. We use this information only to help you.

5. How long we keep data

Data stored on your device stays until you log out, delete your account or uninstall the app. Records we must keep by law, such as safety incident reports, are kept only as long as required and then deleted.

6. Your choices and account deletion

  • Use Rydo in guest mode without giving a name or phone number.
  • Delete your account anytime: Rydo → Account → Delete account. See the account deletion page.
  • Ask us for a copy of your data or to correct it by emailing us.

7. Children

Rydo is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. Riders must be adults with a valid driving licence.

8. Security

Data sent over the internet uses encrypted connections (HTTPS). Your phone number is never shown to riders.

9. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the effective date above. Important changes will also be shown in the app.
