FAQ

Questions, answered

Where does Rydo work?

Rydo is launching in Kohat, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, covering about 12 km around the city.

How is the fare decided?

Rydo shows a suggested fare for your distance. You can offer more or less. Riders accept your price or send a counter-offer, and you pick the one you like.

Is my phone number shared with the rider?

No. Calls and chats happen inside the app, so your number stays private.

Can I use Rydo without an account?

Yes. You can explore in guest mode. Log in with your phone number when you're ready to save your rides.

How do I delete my account?

Open Rydo → Account → Delete account. Or see our account deletion page.
