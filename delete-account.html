Delete your Rydo account

App: Rydo · Developer: Global Nova Technologies

Delete in the app

  1. Open Rydo and go to Account (riders: Profile).
  2. Scroll down and tap Delete account.
  3. Read what will be deleted and tap Delete to confirm. Your account and data are erased right away.

Can't open the app?

Email ahmiikhanx885@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Rydo account" and the phone number on your account. We will confirm and delete it within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your profile: name and phone number
  • Ride history and saved places
  • Favourite riders and emergency contacts
  • Chat messages
  • App settings (language, theme)
  • For riders: your rider application, documents and earnings history

What may be kept

Records we are legally required to keep, such as reports about safety incidents, may be kept for the period required by law and then deleted. They are not used for any other purpose.
